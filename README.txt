Imperatores Invictissimis - Imperator Rome
This is a mod made for the Imperator Rome that adds more diplomatic actions for players to allow for more types of interations between Player countries

Send custom amount of gold to a player
Send custom amount of manpower to a player
Demand a player country convert to your religion
Demand a player become multiple types of subjects
Offer truce action

Allow ruler change character action on your ruler
  this lets other players select a character in your or their country to install as your ruler
